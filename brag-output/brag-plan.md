# Brag Plan: Nava

## What is this app?
Nava turns an Algerian student's transcript (or their bac results, via a chatbot) into a real, dated, document-by-document plan to study — first in Algeria, then abroad — against 20+ human-curated scholarships, with every fact linked to its official source.

## The angle
A pitch a founder can show a business person in under a minute: **the information layer Algerian students are missing.** Not another AI chatbot — a curated, deterministic engine ("CURATED, NOT GENERATED") that reads a student's real grades and produces the one thing nobody gives them: a plan. The joke-free, premium, serious read is the point — this is a serious product.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The app's own headline at full scale on the mint canvas: **"Your transcript already contains a plan."** Then the second half lands: **"We read it and build the steps."** Serif, calm, confident.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The problem, stated as numbers:** 12 Algerian procedures, 20+ scholarship deadlines, one confused student. Deadline cards stack with real-looking labels.
2. **The product working (centerpiece):** the three-beat user flow recreated as UI —
   - *Upload:* a transcript card lands; an OCR progress bar fills to 100%;
   - *Match:* verdict chips (Eligible / Likely / Stretch) stamp onto scholarship cards;
   - *Plan:* a dated timeline unrolls with a document checklist ticking over.
3. **The trust contract:** verbatim — "We never invent a deadline, a fee or a test minimum. Unverified facts are marked and treated as unknown." Plus the banner: "CURATED, NOT GENERATED — every fact links to its official source and verification date."
4. **Breadth:** 20+ curated scholarships · 12 Algerian procedures · 3 languages (EN/FR/AR full parity). Two doors: "I just got my bac" (Algerian orientation) and "I'm studying at a university" (abroad).

## Outro / punchline
Logo + the app's own tagline: **"From a photo of your transcript to a real plan to study abroad."** Long hold. Silence over the last chord.

## User flow worth showing
Photo of transcript → deterministic match with verdict chips → tracked application with a dated plan and document checklist. This is the centerpiece (Scene 3), recreated as working UI, not described.
